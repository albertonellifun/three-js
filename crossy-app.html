🥊😼Rocky Graffietto Edition Quick Guide

Rocky Graffietto is a street-smart cat with 9 lives and a desperate need for personal space. Navigate the urban chaos one block at a time: dodge speed-demon commuters, leap across river logs, and play high-stakes chicken with incoming trains. Push as far away from home as your paws can carry you—partly for the ultimate bragging rights, but mostly because the vet is still looking for you. One wrong jump and it's right back to the scratching post!
